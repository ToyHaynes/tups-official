A rhythm laboratory

One small idea unfolds into
hundreds of possibilities.

Tups doesn't hand you a method. It opens up the subdivisions, the syncopations and the tuplets, and lets you hear what lives between the beats.

01 · TUPS

Every subdivision
from 2 to 12.

Every tuplet from 2 to 12 in a single interface. Tap any note to mute or activate it: the fastest way into 5s, 7s and 11s.

  • PossibilitiesEvery syncopation of tuplets 2 to 12. The dice fills your sequence with new ones.
  • Arrange & ComposeTurn single cells into phrases, then play them back as a line.
  • Nested tupletsSplit one note into a tuplet of its own: quintuplets inside a triplet.

02 · A&R+

Sticking practice,
reimagined.

An interactive sticking workspace. Place accents on tuplet notes to write a melody, then hear how it changes with different stickings.

  • Shadow, Mirror, OnlyThree new variations for every sticking.
  • Grouping generatorPick a group length and tap the accented notes, like 1 3 4 of 5.
  • Up to 16 barsSingle, Double, Paradiddle or your own, bar by bar.
03 · PATTERN

One phrase,
every meter.

Take a small idea. Play it in any tuplet, start it anywhere, end it anywhere. Feel how the same phrase lives in 5/8, 7/8, a triplet, a quintuplet.

  • Drummer ModeR / L / K / H notation for any sticking.
  • Melodic Mode39 scales on any root, with piano and guitar views.
  • Dual ScaleThe same pattern across two scales, keys or modes.

04 · TUP LAB

Polyrhythms, polymeters
and grooves.

A four-channel drum machine. Each channel runs its own tuplet, sound and pattern, all locked to one master tempo. Build 7 against 4. Build 5:3:7.

  • Four viewsLine, Circle, Geometry and Sigil, where your rhythm draws itself as a mandala.
  • Metric ModulationTwo pulses, A and B, joined by a fader or a program. The tempo shifts by the exact ratio.
  • Polyrhythm ModeSpeed a polyrhythm up until it crosses into pitch, and the ratio becomes a chord.
  • RHYTHMS90 world rhythms from 12 regions, 10 song grooves, 63 polyrhythm chords, 12 modulations.
  • 25 soundsDrum kit, cymbals, hand drums like conga and darbuka, percussion and mallets.

Questions

Good to know

Is Tups only for drummers?

No. PATTERN's Melodic Mode has 39 scales with piano and guitar views, and polyrhythms, odd groupings and metric modulation matter on every instrument. Plenty of people practice with Tups on their knees.

What can I do for free?

The full metronome, Practice Tracker, every tuplet from 2 to 12 in TUPS and PATTERN, and the first steps of A&R+ and TUP LAB. No ads, no time limit. See Free & Pro.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your phone, with no account and no connection needed.

How accurate is the timing?

Tups uses its own native audio engine on iOS and Android. Clicks are placed to about 20 microseconds, with zero drift from 10 to 500 BPM.

Can I share what I make?

With Pro you can save presets in every module, send them as a link that opens straight in Tups, and export MIDI from all four modules.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play, so you can cancel anytime in your store account. Lifetime is a single purchase with nothing to cancel.

Where can I learn the modules?

Every module has a guide behind its ⓘ button in the app. There's also a guide page here, and you can always write to support@tupstheapp.com.
