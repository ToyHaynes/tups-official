Hear Polyrhythms as Chords

Every polyrhythm is a chord waiting to be heard. Take a 4:5:6 and speed it up past the point where your ear can count the pulses — rhythm crosses into pitch. The ratio becomes an interval. The pattern becomes harmony.

That's Pitch Engine: 43 chord presets built on polyrhythm ratios. Slow it down and it's rhythm. Speed it up and it's a chord. Same math — your ear just changes how it listens.

One Lab, Four Modules — and the Metronome Underneath

Each module asks the same question from a different angle: how many ways can a beat be divided — and what does each one feel like?

🥁

TUPS

The Space Between the Beats
Every subdivision from 2 to 12 in one interface — quintuplets, septuplets and beyond. Tap any note to mute or activate it and hear every syncopation a single beat can hold. Possibilities (Pro) lays out every variation as a grid you can sequence with Arrange and Compose.
🎵

A&R+

Sticking Practice, Reimagined
An interactive sticking workspace. Place accents on tuplet notes to write your own line, then hear it transform with Shadow, Mirror and Only — or save your own stickings. Classic methodology, rebuilt as something you can touch.
📊

PATTERN

Familiar Motifs, Infinite Possibilities
Write one short pattern, then watch it open. Drummer mode plays R / L / K / H as four drum sounds; Melodic mode plays the same idea across 39 scales, shown in real time on a piano keyboard or guitar fretboard. Dual Scale (Pro) alternates one pattern between two scales.
🔬

TUP LAB

Polyrhythms & Grooves
A four-channel drum machine — each channel its own tuplet, sound and pattern, all locked to one tempo. Build 7 against 4, build 5:3:7. Polyrhythm Mode (Pro) shows it as a Clock, an Orbit, or pushes it into Pitch to hear it as a chord. Per-channel swing (50–66%) for jazz, blues, shuffle and funk.
⏱️

THE METRONOME

The Foundation. Free.
A Full Screen Metronome with Pendulum or Orbit visuals, Tap Tempo, per-beat accents, 15 click sounds and time signatures from 2/4 to 12/4. Silent Practice (Pro) drops the click at intervals to build your inner clock.

Full how-to guides live inside the app — tap ⓘ in any module, or open Guide in Settings.

How the Lab Works

1 · Start where you're curious

Pick a module based on today's question — a tuplet you can't feel yet, a sticking, a polyrhythm, a scale.

2 · Make it yours

Mute notes, place accents, layer channels, write melodies. Sculpt the pattern until it's exactly the one you need.

3 · Take it to your instrument

Save it, share it as a .tups file, and bring the idea to your kit, your keys or your fretboard. Every experiment becomes part of your vocabulary.

Rhythm Isn't an Answer. It's a Question.

I've been a working jazz drummer for nearly two decades, and my practice room has always been a strange laboratory — half sweat, half questions. How does 5 sit inside 7? What does the pocket feel like in 11/8? I didn't need the answers handed to me. I needed a space to hear them, write them down and keep them.

So I built Tups. Not a metronome — a rhythm laboratory. Four modules, each asking the same question from a different angle. It won't tell you how "real" musicians play; it opens things up — subdivisions, scales, stickings, tuplets — and says: try it, listen, try again.

I wrote the code alone. No ads, no tracking, fully offline, a native engine that doesn't drift to 500 BPM. None of that is marketing — those are just the things that drove me crazy in other apps.

Tups will never be "finished." Neither are my questions. Let's keep experimenting.

— Batuhan, Denizli

Questions, Answered

What exactly is Tups?

A rhythm laboratory: four modules (TUPS, A&R+, PATTERN, TUP LAB) and a reference-grade metronome. Metronomes keep time; Tups lets you take rhythm apart — every tuplet from 2 to 12, polyrhythms, odd time, 39 scales — and rebuild it your way.

Is it just another metronome?

The metronome is the foundation, not the product. It's free, accurate to ±20 microseconds, and would be a solid app on its own. The four modules are what happens after the metronome ends.

I'm not a drummer. Is this for me?

Yes. PATTERN's Melodic mode plays any pattern in 39 scales, shown on a piano keyboard or guitar fretboard in real time. Pianists, guitarists, horn players, producers — anyone who works with rhythm works with Tups.

Do I need to know music theory?

No. Everything is visual and audible — you tap, you listen, you adjust. If you do know theory, there's plenty to dig into: 39 scales, dual-scale alternation, polyrhythm ratios that turn into chords.

What's free and what's Pro?

Free includes the full-precision metronome, the Practice Tracker, the Melodic Visualizer and all four modules at core level. Pro unlocks Polyrhythm Mode, Grouping, Dual Scale, Arrange & Compose, Silent Practice, unlimited tuplets/BPM/bars, and saving & sharing.

How does the free trial work?

Pick a monthly or yearly plan and the first 7 days are free — cancel anytime before it ends and you pay nothing. If it lapses, the free tier stays fully usable and your saved patterns are kept, not deleted.

Does it work offline?

Completely. No account required, no internet required, and audio keeps running in the background. Practice rooms, planes, basements — all fine.

What happens to my data?

Almost nothing, because we collect almost nothing. No ads, no tracking. Your patterns live on your device, and you share them as .tups files only when you choose to.
