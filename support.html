Quick Help

Getting Started

  • Download from the App Store or Google Play.
  • The metronome is the foundation — start there, then open the four modules.
  • Use headphones for the most accurate sound.
  • Tap ⓘ inside any module for a quick guide, or open Guide in Settings.

Common Issues

  • No sound? Check device volume and that Tups has audio permission.
  • App crashes? Restart it, and make sure you're on the latest version.
  • Subscription trouble? Check your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Restoring Pro? Settings → restore purchases, with the same store account you subscribed on.
